Services

Good design does not always begin with demolition.

Sometimes it begins by finishing the room properly. Allison McElroy Design offers a practical path from simple refresh to full renovation design. Each service starts by understanding the home, the people who live there and what should stay.

How services are delivered

In person

Orange County, California. Best suited to home refresh sessions, hands-on styling and organizing, site-dependent design work, measurements and local kitchen, bath and renovation projects.

Remote

Available for select services, especially consultations, color and materials direction, decorating plans, layout and design guidance, phased planning and design support for your local contractor or trades.

Remote projects depend on accurate photos, measurements and information from you and, when construction is involved, a qualified local team. Hands-on organizing or installation is not included remotely unless separately arranged.

Home Refresh: Decorating, Styling & Organizing

Orange County · In Person · Remote plan available

A reasonable, lower-commitment way to make a home feel pulled together before deciding on a full renovation.

Use what you have first. Buy only what the room is missing.

What is included

  • Assessment of furniture, art, accessories and storage you already own
  • Furniture rearrangement and room-layout recommendations
  • A coordinated color direction based on existing finishes and undertones
  • Editing and styling shelves, surfaces and focal points
  • Storage and organizing plan for the room
  • A short, focused list of gaps to buy, only where new pieces will make a real difference
  • Optional hands-on styling and organizing session

Best for

Families who like much of what they own but cannot make the room feel finished; families considering a renovation who want to test how far decorating can take the space first.

Color & Materials Direction

In Person / Remote

Confident, timeless selections that coordinate with the home’s fixed elements.

What is included

  • Paint color direction
  • Undertone review of flooring, counters, tile, cabinetry and other fixed finishes
  • Coordination of wood tones, metals, fabrics and accent colors
  • Palette for one room or connected spaces
  • Guidance on what to keep, paint, replace or postpone

Best for

Clients who feel their colors and finishes are fighting each other, or who want a plan before purchasing furniture, tile, counters or fixtures.

Kitchen, Bath & Renovation Design

Orange County · In Person · Remote by Project

Architecturally informed design for families who are ready to renovate now, or who need a phased plan for later.

Included, as appropriate to the project

  • Space planning
  • Cabinet and storage planning
  • Floor plans and elevations
  • Finish, fixture and lighting selections
  • Color and materials coordination
  • Design drawings and presentation visuals
  • A phased roadmap when the full project will happen over time

Allison provides design services. Construction is performed by your licensed contractor and trades.

Designed & Organized

Design and organizing together, so new or refreshed spaces work beautifully from the first day.

Organizing consultant

Brenda McElroy, CPO®, Organized By Choice (opens in a new tab).

Possible focus areas

  • Kitchens and pantries
  • Closets
  • Mudrooms and entry storage
  • Paper and household systems
  • Move-in setup
  • Post-renovation organization
  • Storage designed around real categories and routines

Clear zones, labels and visual calm, adapted to real families, ordinary budgets and the belongings you already own. Not every solution needs a full matching set of new containers.

Process

Good choices, in the right order.

  1. Discover

    Walk through the home, routines, frustrations and goals.

  2. Start with what exists

    Identify what stays and what the new design must work with.

  3. Plan in phases

    Separate quick improvements from later renovation work.

  4. Design

    Coordinate layout, color, materials, lighting and storage.

  5. Organize & style

    Put systems in place and finish the room properly.

  6. Renovate when ready

    If construction is still needed, proceed with a clearer and more confident plan.
