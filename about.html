About

Modern by training. Timeless by conviction.

Allison McElroy is a designer with a master’s in architecture and professional architecture and marketing experience. Based in Costa Mesa, she brings that combination of design training, architectural practice and marketing work to a practical, visually clear approach to homes.

How the style has evolved

Allison’s style was once very modern. Today it pairs clean lines and calm color with classic materials, natural texture and rooms planned around the way families actually live. She is less interested in chasing trends than in helping clients make choices they will still love years from now.
